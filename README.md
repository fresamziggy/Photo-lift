# Photo Lift

Upscale, relight and sharpen photos entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and it works offline once installed.

**Open the app:** https://fresamziggy.github.io/photo-lift/

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Photo Lift from the home screen once while online. After that it works in airplane mode.

On Windows, Mac or Android, open the link in Chrome or Edge and choose **Install app** from the menu or address bar.

## Files
- `photo-lift.html` is the app source.
- `python3 build.py` rebuilds the installable app into `docs/` (page, manifest, icons, offline service worker). GitHub Pages serves `docs/`.
